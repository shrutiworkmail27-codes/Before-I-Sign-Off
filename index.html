01 Desk Mechanics

The "F9" Reflex

02 Email Etiquette

"Per My Last Email"

"Noted with thanks" means read and ignored. "Re-attaching for visibility" means look before you ask. "As discussed" translates directly to: I have documented this so you cannot deny it later.

04 Biological Law
